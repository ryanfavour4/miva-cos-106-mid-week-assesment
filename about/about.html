About Me

Who I am, where I've been, where I'm going

A short introduction to my background, education, skills, and the things that keep me curious outside of coursework.

Hello, I'm Ryan

I'm Ryan Favour Chukwuka, a 200-level Computer Science student at MIVA Open University. I grew up curious about how things work — taking apart gadgets, then websites, then eventually learning to build them myself.

What started as a hobby — tweaking HTML on a school blog — has grown into a genuine interest in software engineering and educational technology. I believe the best tools are the ones that make learning feel less like a chore, and I try to build with that in mind.

Outside of coding, I enjoy writing, teaching friends what I've learned, and following developments in open-source software.

Career Aspirations

Where I want technology to take me

I see myself growing into a full-stack developer who can build end-to-end products — not just write code, but understand the people who use it. My time at MIVA has shown me how powerful digital learning can be, and I'm drawn to projects that improve access to education.

In the near term, I want to gain internship experience at a technology company where I can work alongside experienced engineers. I'm particularly interested in backend development and developer experience — the tools that make other developers more productive.

Longer term, I'd like to contribute to open-source projects and eventually build educational software that serves Nigerian students studying at a distance, like I do.
